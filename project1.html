Food Delivery App UX Research

Improving delivery experience and reducing user frustration

🧠 Project Overview

This project focuses on identifying and solving user confusion during the food ordering process, especially related to delivery confirmation, delays, and service availability.

🎯 Goal

To improve clarity, prevent failed orders, and enhance trust in the delivery experience.

📖 Real User Experience

While ordering food, I entered my home address and placed the order successfully. However, after reaching a nearby location, the delivery person informed me that my area was outside their service limit. This created confusion and frustration because the app initially allowed the order.

👥 Users

I spoke with 6 users who frequently use food delivery apps.

🔍 Research Method

  • User interviews
  • Observation of app usage
  • Quick survey

📊 Key Findings

  • Users can place orders even when they are outside the delivery service area
  • Service limitations are not clearly communicated before ordering
  • Issues are discovered too late in the process
  • This leads to frustration and loss of trust

💬 User Quote

"Why did the app let me order if it can’t deliver to my location?"

💡 Insights

  • Users expect service availability to be confirmed before placing an order
  • Late-stage failure creates a poor user experience
  • Clear system feedback is essential to avoid confusion

🛠️ Solution

  • Show delivery availability before allowing users to place an order
  • Provide real-time location validation
  • Display clear warning messages for non-serviceable areas
  • Improve tracking clarity with simple visual updates

🔄 Before vs After

Before: User places order → Order accepted → Delivery fails ❌

After: User enters address → System checks availability → Order allowed or blocked ✅
